Contact us

Email ahmetulutas93@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and which iOS version you have, and what happened.

Gloop says it cannot see

Hue Chew needs the camera to play. If the camera was turned off, open the Grown-ups screen, answer the question and tap Open Settings. In Settings, switch Camera on for Hue Chew, then go back to the app. You can also find it in the iPhone Settings app, under Hue Chew.

Sound

The sound switch is on the Grown-ups screen. Gloop is also quiet when the silent switch on the iPhone is on.

Starting again

To delete every creature, picture and bit of progress, open the Grown-ups screen and choose Reset all data. To remove just one creature, open it in the Sticker Book and hold Delete for a couple of seconds.

The Grown-ups screen

It is behind a simple question for grown-ups. It holds the sound switch, Open Settings, the privacy policy link and Reset all data.
