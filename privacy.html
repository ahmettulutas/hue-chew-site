Hue Chew privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Hue Chew is a color-hunting game for young children. Gloop, a hungry blob, asks for a color. Your child points the iPhone camera at something of that color and Gloop eats it. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with information. It is written for parents and carers.

In short: Hue Chew has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code. The app has no way to send anything over the internet. Pictures stay on this iPhone.

Children

Hue Chew is made for children aged 5 and under and is listed in the App Store as Made for Kids. We do not ask your child, or you, for a name, an email address or any other personal details. There is nothing to sign up for.

The camera

The camera is used only on the Hunt screen, to see the colors around you. The app looks at the camera picture on the device itself to work out which color it is. Nothing from the camera is recorded as video, and the camera is off on every other screen.

When Gloop eats a bite, the app takes a small square picture from the middle of the camera view (a crop, 256 by 256 pixels). The crop is kept in memory while a round is played. If your child leaves a round early, those crops are thrown away. When a creature finishes hatching (after 5 bites), its 5 crops are saved on this iPhone so they can show up in Gloop's colored bands in the Sticker Book.

These pictures are never uploaded or sent anywhere. The app does not use your Photos library and does not save anything to it.

What is saved on this iPhone

Everything the game remembers lives in the app's own storage on this iPhone:

  • A small file called save.json. It holds the creature names Gloop makes up, the colors of each bite, which special recipes your child has found, how many rounds were played, and whether sound is on or off.
  • The saved crop pictures described above, in a separate folder.

Backups

The app never sends pictures anywhere, and it asks iOS to keep the crop pictures out of iPhone backups. The save.json file is not excluded, so creature names and the game's progress can be part of your own iPhone backup (for example iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer), if you use one. That backup belongs to you and is handled by Apple and your own settings, not by the app.

What we do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No ads and no advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics and no usage statistics.
  • No tracking, within the app or across other apps and websites.
  • No third party code or services inside the app.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No microphone and no speech recognition. Gloop's voice is made by the iPhone itself.

The Grown-ups screen

The Grown-ups screen is behind a simple question that is meant to stop young children from opening it. It is the only place in the app with links that leave the game: Open Settings (to turn the camera back on) and a link to this policy. It also holds the sound switch and Reset all data.

Deleting information

  • One creature: open it in the Sticker Book and hold the Delete button for a couple of seconds. The creature and its 5 pictures are removed.
  • Everything: in the Grown-ups screen choose Reset all data and confirm. This deletes all creatures, all saved pictures and all progress.
  • Deleting the app from the iPhone removes its saved information too. Copies inside a backup you made earlier stay in that backup until you delete the backup.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change.
